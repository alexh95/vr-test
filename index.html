Skyreach

A floating-island VR playground. Built for the Steam Frame.

Controls
  • Grip: grab, hold, throw. Point at something far away, hold grip and flick your wrist back to pull it to you.
  • Trigger: use what you hold (paint with a brush, light a rocket).
  • Right stick: push forward to aim a teleport, release to jump. Left/right to snap-turn.
  • Left stick: walk smoothly.
  • Look at your left palm for the wrist menu. Poke it with your right index finger, or point and pull the trigger.
Running this on a Steam Frame
  • Stream from this PC: start SteamVR, open this page in Chrome or Edge on the desktop, and press Enter VR.
  • Standalone needs a WebXR-capable browser on the headset. See the README.
  • No headset? Add ?emulate to the URL for an emulated one.
